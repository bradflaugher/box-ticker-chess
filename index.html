    The fine print

    1. The pieces move exactly as in ordinary chess — castling, en passant and promotion included.
    2. Three-Check (Form 3-C): each check ticks one of your three boxes; tick all three to win immediately. A double check still ticks one box.
    3. King of the Hill (Form K-H): march your king legally onto any of the four central squares (d4, e4, d5, e5) to win immediately.
    4. Checkmate always wins in both modes, and you may never leave your own king in check.
    5. Stalemate, threefold repetition and fifty quiet moves are draws (bare kings is a draw in Three-Check only).

    Tip: in Three-Check, keep your king tucked away; in King of the Hill, escort your king boldly toward the summit.
