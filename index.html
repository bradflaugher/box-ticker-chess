    The fine print

    1. The pieces move exactly as in ordinary chess — castling, en passant and promotion included.
    2. Each time you put the enemy king in check, one of your three boxes is ticked.
    3. Tick all three and you win immediately, whatever else is on the board.
    4. Checkmate still wins too, and a single move that attacks the king twice only ticks one box.
    5. You may not leave your own king in check. Stalemate, threefold repetition and fifty quiet moves are draws.

    Tip: a piece sacrificed for a check is often a bargain. Keep your king tucked away.
